Good technical notes begin with a real problem. They explain what mattered, what did not, and why the final solution worked.

That is the aim of this blog. I'll use it to collect practical lessons from working with Oracle Database and APEX--from focused SQL and PL/SQL techniques to broader thoughts on designing applications that remain understandable over time.

The goal is simple: concise articles, useful examples, and ideas that can be applied to real work.
